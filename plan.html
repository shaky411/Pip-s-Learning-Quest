One-day lesson plan: Year 4 (age 8–9)

Aligned to the England National Curriculum, Key Stage 2 (lower). Children aged 8 are usually in Year 3 or Year 4; this plan targets Year 4 and works as consolidation for Year 3 (choose fewer tables, skip 24-hour time). Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland follow different curricula, but the skills here are broadly similar.

How to use it

  • Open the site on a tablet, laptop or phone. Your child enters their name and follows the quests in order (they can swap them around).
  • Sit with them for the first quest, then step back. Ask them to explain an answer they got wrong; talking it through is where the learning happens.
  • Before or during a quest, use the 📚 Learn Zone (the Learn link at the top). It has interactive guides with examples for fractions, Roman numerals, times tables, place value, telling the time, grammar, spelling, Roman Britain and science. Your child can play with sliders and see how things work.
  • The suggested times are a guide only. Short bursts of 20–30 minutes with movement between work to suit an 8-year-old's attention.
  • Have paper, a pencil, a ruler and a clock (analogue, if you have one) nearby for the offline extensions below.
  • Stars, stickers and the certificate are saved in this browser only; nothing is sent anywhere. Use “Start a new day” at the bottom of the home page to reset.

Reading the results

  • 7+ right: secure. Celebrate and move on.
  • 4–6 right: developing. Replay the quest tomorrow.
  • Under 4: stop and teach it together with real objects, then replay.
  • Replaying always generates fresh questions, so it's good for practice.

Adjusting the difficulty

  • Easier: choose only the 2, 5, 10, 3 and 4 times tables.
  • Harder: choose every table, then try beating your score.
  • Stuck on a spelling? The site makes them type it once to practise.
  • Do the day across two days if you'd like a gentler pace.

A note on screen time and sound

Sound effects and spoken spelling words can be switched off with the speaker button at the top. Spoken words use your device's built-in voice, so they work best on up-to-date Chrome, Safari or Edge. Each quest takes about 10–20 minutes, with movement breaks and offline extensions in between.
