# 🦊 Pip's Learning Quest

**A fun, game-style day of learning at home for 8-year-olds (Year 4, UK National Curriculum).**

### 👉 [Play it now: shaky411.github.io/Pip-s-Learning-Quest](https://shaky411.github.io/Pip-s-Learning-Quest/)

Works on any tablet, laptop or phone. No sign-up, no adverts, no tracking.

---

## What is it?

Pip the fox guides your child through a day of bite-sized "quests". Each one earns stars and a sticker, and there's a certificate at the end. Collect enough stars to level up!

| Time | Quest | Subject |
|------|-------|---------|
| 09:00 | 🚀 Times Table Blast-Off | Maths |
| 09:30 | 🍕 Pizza Fractions | Maths |
| 10:10 | 🤸 Brain Break | Movement |
| 10:30 | 🕵️ Spelling Spies | English |
| 11:00 | 💪 Grammar Gym | English |
| 11:30 | 🔍 Reading Detective | English |
| 12:15 | 🥪 Lunch & Play | Break |
| 13:15 | ⏰ Number & Time Detectives | Maths |
| 13:45 | 🏛️ Roman Britain Time Travel | History |
| 14:15 | 🎨 Roman Mosaic Maker | Art |
| 14:45 | 🧪 Science Lab: Solid, Liquid, Gas | Science |
| 15:15 | ✍️ Story Studio | English |
| Bonus | ⚡ Electric Circuits · 🗺️ Explore the UK | Science · Geography |

The times are only a guide, so your child can do the quests in any order and at their own pace.

## 📚 Learn Zone

Stuck, or want to warm up first? The **Learn** link at the top opens a set of hands-on guides, with sliders and examples, for:

- Times tables: arrays, fact families and tricks
- Fractions: pizza builder, equivalent fractions, fractions of an amount
- Place value and rounding
- Roman numerals: the rules, a converter and a decoder
- Telling the time: a clock with 12-hour and 24-hour time
- Grammar and spelling cheat sheets
- Roman Britain timeline
- Science: states of matter and conductors

Every quest also has a **📚 Need a hint?** button that jumps to the matching guide.

## For parents

- 📋 **Lesson plan:** the site has a printable [lesson plan page](https://shaky411.github.io/Pip-s-Learning-Quest/plan.html) with National Curriculum links, learning goals and offline activities for each quest.
- 🔊 **Sound:** the speaker button at the top turns sound effects and spoken spelling words on or off.
- 🔁 **Replays:** questions are different every time, so repeating a quest is good practice.
- 👋 **Change name or start a new day:** use the links at the bottom of the home page.
- 🔒 **Privacy:** stars, stickers and stories are saved only in your browser on that device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Tips

- Sit with your child for the first quest, then step back.
- If something is tricky, talk it through together and then replay it.
- Add the site to your home screen or bookmarks so it's one tap away.

## Run it on your own computer

Open a terminal in this folder and run one of these, then visit the address it shows:

```
python -m http.server 8000
```
or
```
npx live-server
```

(For the first command, visit http://localhost:8000.)
